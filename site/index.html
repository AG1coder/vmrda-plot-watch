Across 134 residential plot listings sampled in the VMRDA belt, the average asking price is roughly ₹20,550 per square yard. But the market is not one market. The most expensive mandal in our sample, Visakhapatnam city (GVMC), commands a median of ₹34,431/sq yd — about 3.3× that of Pendurthi, the least expensive.

The story of this market in a single number is Bhogapuram, where the new international airport is under construction. Its median asking price of ₹15,250/sq yd already trails the Visakhapatnam city core by a wide margin — land is being priced on expectation. Speculative buyers are positioning along the NH-16 corridor that ties these districts together.

Farther down the coast, Anakapalle — the industrial and pharma hub of the Anakapalli district — trades at a more grounded ₹11,515/sq yd. Its demand is driven less by speculation and more by working households and the plants at Atchutapuram and Parawada. The spread between the city core and the corridor towns is where most of the investment debate sits.

Methodology & caveats

This page is regenerated automatically once a month. Each run fetches residential plot/land listings from real-estate aggregators, tiles them onto the 16 VMRDA mandals of interest, filters clearly-erroneous entries, and samples up to 20 listings per mandal.

  • Plots only. This view covers residential building plots; large land parcels (typically >1 acre / 4,840 sq yd) are excluded so farmland and multi-acre sites don't skew the per-square-yard picture.
  • Freshness. Every listing carries a last-updated date (1acre.in verified timestamps; realestateindia "Posted on" dates). The band above shows what fraction of the sampled listings were updated within the last 90 days. Hover any marker/dot to see when that listing was last refreshed.
  • Asking, not transacted. Prices are advertised asking prices; final deals may differ.
  • Sample sizes vary. Mandals with fewer than 3 usable listings are omitted from the chart (see table for n).
  • Units. All prices normalized to rupees per square yard (1 sq yd = 9 sq ft).
  • Outlier handling. Listings below ₹800/sq yd or above ₹100,000/sq yd are treated as artifacts and excluded.
  • Coverage. Live sources are realestateindia (listing feeds) and 1acre.in (verified lands/plots, incl. VMRDA master-plan, airport-road and beach-corridor layers). The major portals (Housing, 99acres, MagicBricks, SquareYards) block automated access and are attached to the pipeline as adapters for when it runs through a residential proxy.
  • Read it directionally. These are monthly market snapshots, not valuations.
