A Monthly Read on Visakhapatnam's Land Market

By VMRDA Plot Watch · August 2026 · Data-backed, auto-generated monthly

There is a simple way to read the Visakhapatnam Metropolitan Region's residential land market this month: priced to promise. The Bhogapuram international airport, under construction a little north of the city, has turned a once-sleepy stretch of the NH-16 corridor into the region's most watched speculation play.

Bhogapuram's median asking price of ₹15,250 per square yard is already well above the Vizianagaram district average of ₹15,250/sq yd. That premium is not about buildings — it is about a runway. Land here is being bought on the expectation of the airport's opening, its special economic zone, and the hotels and logistics that follow.

The contrast with Anakapalle, the industrial anchor of the Anakapalli district, is instructive. There the median sits at ₹11,515/sq yd — roughly 0.8× cheaper than Bhogapuram. Its buyers are owners and workers tied to the Atchutapuram and Parawada plants, not speculators. One corridor, two entirely different demand curves.

Methodology

Each month the pipeline fetches residential plot/land listings, samples up to 20 per mandal, normalizes prices to ₹/sq yd, and drops outliers outside ₹800–₹100,000/sq yd. Figures are asking prices, not transactions, and are directional. Source feeds: realestateindia: 243, 1acre: 39.
